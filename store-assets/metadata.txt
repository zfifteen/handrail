Subtitle:

Promotional text:
Supervise local Grok Build sessions from your iPhone while your Mac stays in control.

Description:
Handrail is a local-first iPhone companion for Grok Build on your Mac. Pair your iPhone with the Handrail desktop CLI on the same local network, then view Grok sessions, follow task progress, continue existing chats, approve tool requests, and stop running work from your phone.

Handrail does not run a cloud workspace, does not require an account, and does not upload your chat data to a Handrail server. Grok Build remains local and authoritative on your Mac. The iPhone app connects to the Handrail CLI over your local Wi-Fi or LAN using a per-device pairing token.

Handrail works with the official Grok Build CLI. Handrail is not affiliated with xAI.

Review notes:
Handrail requires Local Network access to connect to the Handrail CLI running on the user's Mac. The connection uses ws:// on the local network and is protected by a per-device pairing token. No Handrail backend account, sync service, payment, telemetry, or cloud relay is used.
